What is VibeCoding Club?

We are a club at Menlo School that builds real apps and games together using AI coding tools. You say what you want, the AI writes the code, and you steer, test and make it yours.

No experience needed. Bring curiosity (and your lunch). Every meeting you show up to earns you points on the scoreboard.

What is vibe coding?

You describe the app you want in normal words. An AI tool like Claude Code, Copilot or ChatGPT writes the code. You run it, see what happens, and keep asking for changes until it feels right. You learn by doing, and by reading what the AI made.

An example conversation

You: “Make a page with a button that plays a sound.”

AI: writes the code ⌨️

You: “Make the button huge and purple.”

That is the whole trick. Repeat until it is awesome.

FAQ

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Most of us started here with zero experience. If you can describe an idea in words, you can vibe code.

Do I need my own laptop?

A school laptop or any computer with a browser works. Most AI tools run right in the browser, so there is nothing to install on day one.

Which AI tools do we use?

Any of them: Claude Code, GitHub Copilot, ChatGPT, Cursor and more. The club has no required tool. Try a couple and keep the one you like.

Does it cost anything?

No. The club is free, and the AI tools we use all have free plans. Some give students even more for free.

Can I come to just some meetings?

Yes. Come when you can. You still earn points for every meeting you attend.

How do I get on the scoreboard?

Come to a meeting, demo something on a WORK day, or help build this website. The README on GitHub shows how to make your first change.
